Design Description

This optimized D-He3 Field-Reversed Configuration (FRC) uses a cylindrical plasma chamber with radius 0.20 m and length 6.54 m, confined by a 5.0 T axial magnetic field. The plasma operates at an ion temperature of 10.0 keV and a D:He3 ratio of 1:5.00, with propellant injected at 1.759 kg/s. The beta-limited plasma pressure is balanced by the magnetic pressure, and the electron temperature is solved to satisfy heating-cooling equilibrium (alpha + auxiliary heating vs bremsstrahlung and ion-electron drag). Charged fusion products are magnetically expanded through the rear nozzle to generate thrust, while the neutron fraction drives the shielding and radiator sizing. Stability is enforced via the S-factor (tilt instability) and elongation (tearing mode) constraints. The system produces ~281,775 N of thrust at ~5,750 s Isp with a total dry mass of ~1,188,309 kg.

  • Synchrotron (Trubnikov): P_syn ∝ n_e B^2 T_e (1 + T_e/204) (1 - R_wall)^0.5
  • Ion gyroradius: rho_i = sqrt(2 * m_i * k_B * T_i) / (q * B)
  • Stability: S = r / rho_i, E = L / (2r)
